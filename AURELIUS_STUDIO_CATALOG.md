# Aurelius Audio Studio: Product Catalog & Technical Documentation
> **"Your assistant in dealing with audio"**

## 1. Executive Summary
Aurelius Audio Studio is a high-performance, AI-driven workspace designed for the modern demands of **Research, Audio Production, and Content Creation**. By leveraging the world-class **Gemini Multimodal Engine**, Aurelius bridges the gap between raw audio and actionable intelligence, providing studio-grade synthesis and forensic-level transcription.

---

## 2. Core Capabilities

### Audio Studio Transcriber (STT)
*Forensic Transcription & Subtitle Generation*
Aurelius provides high-accuracy transcription for live voice recording, local audio files, and streaming video links (up to 120 minutes/200MB).
- **Built-in Live Recorder:** Client-side IndexedDB caching for up to 120 minutes with lossless 16-bit PCM WAV export.
- **Speaker Diarization:** Automatically identifies and labels different speakers (Speaker A, Speaker B, etc.), essential for qualitative research and interview analysis.
- **SRT Subtitle Engine:** Exports production-ready `.srt` files with millisecond-precision timestamps for immediate use in Premiere Pro, Final Cut, or DaVinci Resolve.
- **Intelligent Summarization:** One-click AI summaries that distill hours of audio into key findings, themes, and action items.
- **Preview Safeguard:** Display features a 35% on-screen preview with full 100% text and subtitle downloads.

## 4. Technical Architecture & Security
Aurelius is built on a "Privacy-First" infrastructure:
- **Engine:** Powered by **Gemini 2.5 Multimodal Models**, ensuring state-of-the-art accuracy in both speech-to-text and text-to-speech.
- **Security:** Integrated with **Google Identity Services (SSO)**. Your identity is verified by Google, and all sessions are secured with industrial JWT (JSON Web Tokens).
- **Data Preservation Policy:** Aurelius does not preserve your audio or transcripts on the server. Once a session ends or a tab is closed, the data is wiped. This ensures total confidentiality for sensitive research material.
